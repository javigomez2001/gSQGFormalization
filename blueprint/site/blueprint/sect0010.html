Let \(\beta +\frac1p{\lt}\gamma {\lt}2p+1\), let \(0\le \epsilon \le \min \{ 1,\epsilon _0\} \) where \(\epsilon _0\) is the constant of lemma 8.25 (so that \(\mathcal{R}_\epsilon \) of definition 8.9 is defined), let \(\overline{f}\in C^2(\mathbb {R})\) with \(\overline{f}'\in L^\infty (\mathbb {R})\) and \(\overline{f}''\in L^\infty _{\mathrm{loc}}\), and such that \(|D|^p\overline{f}'\) is defined in the sense of definition 1.5; let \(r\in X_\gamma \). Then \(\mathcal{E}_\epsilon (\overline{f}+r)\) and \(\mathcal{E}_\epsilon (\overline{f})\) are defined, and for every \(y\in \mathbb {R}\)

\[ \mathcal{N}_{\epsilon ,\overline{f}}(r) =\mathcal{E}_\epsilon (\overline{f}+r)-\mathcal{E}_\epsilon (\overline{f})-\mathcal{L}_\epsilon r =\mathcal{N}_1(r)+\mathcal{N}_2(r)+\mathcal{N}_3(r)-\mathcal{R}_\epsilon r . \]

In particular \(\mathcal{N}_{\epsilon ,\overline{f}}(0)=0\).

Proof ▶

Put \(f=\overline{f}+r\); then \(f\in W^{2,\infty }_{\mathrm{loc}}\) and \(f'\in L^\infty \), so proposition 3.10 applies to both \(f\) and \(\overline{f}\):

\[ \mathcal{E}_\epsilon (g)=|D|^pg'+B_\epsilon (g')(g-yg')+\mathcal{I}_\epsilon (g),\qquad g\in \{ \overline{f},\overline{f}+r\} . \]

Step 1 (fractional term). By linearity of \(|D|^p\) on the class of definition 1.5 (which contains \(\overline{f}'\) and \(r'\); note \(r'\in L^1\cap L^\infty \) since admissibility gives \(\gamma {\gt}\beta +1/p{\gt}1+1/p\)), \(|D|^p(\overline{f}'+r')-|D|^p\overline{f}'=|D|^pr'\).

Step 2 (local term). Adding and subtracting \(B_\epsilon (\overline{f}'+r')(\overline{f}-y\overline{f}')\),

\[ B_\epsilon (\overline{f}'+r')(\overline{f}+r-y\overline{f}'-yr')-B_\epsilon (\overline{f}')(\overline{f}-y\overline{f}') =\mathcal{N}_1(r)+B_\epsilon (\overline{f}'+r')(r-yr') =\mathcal{N}_1(r)+\mathcal{N}_2(r)+(r-yr'). \]

Step 3 (nonlocal term). By definition, \(\mathcal{I}_\epsilon (\overline{f}+r)-\mathcal{I}_\epsilon (\overline{f})=\mathcal{N}_3(r)+b_\epsilon r'\).

Step 4 (subtracting \(\mathcal{L}_\epsilon \)). By definition 2.17, \(\mathcal{L}_\epsilon r=|D|^pr'+r-yr'+c_\epsilon \chi _+y^{-p}r'+\mathcal{R}_\epsilon r\), and by lemma 3.12 \(c_\epsilon \chi _+y^{-p}=b_\epsilon \). Summing Steps 1–3 and subtracting: the terms \(|D|^pr'\), \(r-yr'\) and \(b_\epsilon r'\) cancel exactly, leaving \(\mathcal{N}_1(r)+\mathcal{N}_2(r)+\mathcal{N}_3(r)-\mathcal{R}_\epsilon r\).

Since \(\mathcal{N}_j(0)=0\) and \(\mathcal{R}_\epsilon 0=0\), \(\mathcal{N}_{\epsilon ,\overline{f}}(0)=0\).

Remark 10.3
#

The free part \(|D|^pr'+r-yr'\) of \(\mathcal{L}_\epsilon \) is the linear part of the fractional and transport terms at \(B_\epsilon \equiv 1\); the nonperturbative coefficient term \(b_\epsilon r'\) is the “far-field model” part of \(D\mathcal{I}_\epsilon (\overline{f})[r]\) (frozen at the tail \(W\), see lemma 10.30) and is subtracted inside \(\mathcal{N}_3\) precisely because it was moved into \(\mathcal{L}_\epsilon \); the conjugation remainder \(\mathcal{R}_\epsilon \), which is linear, profile-independent, and \(O(\epsilon )\) from \(X_\gamma \) to \(Y_\gamma \) by lemma 8.25, appears with a minus sign. This explains the choice of the decomposition; the later far-field estimates are not used in the algebraic identity above.
