4.1 Standing conventions and auxiliary facts

Standing conventions.

Throughout we fix the parameters of definition 2.1: \(\frac34{\lt}p{\lt}1\), \(\alpha =p+1\), \(\beta =\frac{p+1}{2p}\) (so \(2\beta =1+\frac1p\), \(1{\lt}\beta {\lt}\frac1p\), \(\beta \le p+1\)), the cutoff \(\chi _+\) (\(\chi _+=0\) on \((-\infty ,1]\), \(\chi _+=1\) on \([2,\infty )\)) and \(\Phi _0(\xi )=\frac{\xi |\xi |^p}{p+1}\); note \(\Phi _0'(\xi )=|\xi |^p\), \(\Phi _0\) is odd and \(C^1\) on \(\mathbb {R}\), \(C^\infty \) on \(\mathbb {R}\setminus \{ 0\} \). \(c_\alpha =\Gamma (1+p)\sin (\pi p/2)/\pi \) is the constant of definition 1.5; \(|D|^p g(y)=c_\alpha \int _\mathbb {R}(g(y)-g(z))|y-z|^{-\alpha }\, dz\) for bounded, locally Lipschitz \(g\) (complex-valued \(g\): apply to real and imaginary parts). \(A_\epsilon (u)=(1+\epsilon u^2)^{-\alpha /2}\) (definition 1.10), \(S_f(y,z)\) is the secant (definition 1.3), \(\mathcal A\) is the class of definition 1.4, \(\mathcal{Q}_\epsilon \) is definition 1.23, and \(\mathcal{L}_0 f=|D|^pf'+f-yf'\), \(\widetilde{\mathcal{E}}_\epsilon (f)=\mathcal{L}_0 f+\mathcal{Q}_\epsilon (f)\) (definition 1.27). Fourier transforms follow definition 1.2: \(\widehat f(\xi )=\int f(x)e^{-ix\xi }dx\), \(\check{m}(y)=\frac1{2\pi }\int m(\xi )e^{iy\xi }d\xi \). \(\langle y \rangle =(1+y^2)^{1/2}\). \(W(y):=\max \{ -y,0\} \) (the corner), with \(W'(y):=-\mathbf1_{(-\infty ,0)}(y)\). For \(\epsilon \ge 0\), \(c_\epsilon =c_\alpha \int _0^1[(1+\epsilon t^2)^{-\alpha /2}-1](1-t)^{p-1}dt\) is the constant of lemma 3.12; \(|c_\epsilon |\le C\epsilon \) for \(\epsilon \in [0,1]\), \(c_0=0\).

An admissible constant is a positive number depending only on \(p\), on the fixed cutoffs (\(\chi _+\), the truncation \(\rho \) of definition 4.4, the cutoff \(\chi \) of definition 4.51), on \(\delta ,\epsilon _0\) of definition 4.38 and on the derivative order \(j\), but not on \(\epsilon \in [0,\epsilon _0]\), \(\mu \in \overline{B}\), \(R\ge R_0\), \(Y\), or the spatial variables.

Let \(L\ge 1\), \(m,\sigma \in \mathbb {R}\), \(A,H{\gt}0\), \(b\in C^\infty (\mathbb {R};\mathbb {C})\) with \(b=0\) on \((-\infty ,L]\), and \(q\in C^\infty ((L/2,\infty );\mathbb {R})\). \[ \partial _\xi ^kT_qb(\xi ) =i\sum _{r=0}^{k+1}\binom {k+1}{r}b^{(r)}(\xi )q^{(k+1-r)}(\xi ). \]

\[ |b^{(r)}(\xi )|\le B_rA\xi ^{m-r},\qquad |q^{(r)}(\xi )|\le D_rH\xi ^{-\sigma -r} \quad (\xi \ge L,\ 0\le r\le k+1), \]

\[ |\partial _\xi ^kT_qb(\xi )|\le \left(\sum _{r=0}^{k+1}\binom {k+1}{r}B_rD_{k+1-r}\right) AH\xi ^{m-1-\sigma -k}\qquad (\xi \ge L). \]

Differentiation and multiplication do not enlarge the closed support of \(b\). Apply the ordinary finite Leibniz formula at order \(k+1\). Each product of bounds has exponent \((m-r)+(-\sigma -(k+1-r))=m-1-\sigma -k\); summing gives the explicit constant.

\[ |\partial _\xi ^kT_q^Nb(\xi )| \le E_{N,k}AH^N\xi ^{m-N(1+\sigma )-k}\qquad (\xi \ge L). \]
